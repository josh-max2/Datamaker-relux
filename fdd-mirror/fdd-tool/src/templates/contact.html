Contact

I read every message. Most questions get a reply within 1–2 business days. If you've spotted a data error on a brand page, please include the brand and the field — that gets fixed the fastest.

What I can help with

  • Data corrections. Spot a wrong number or a stale filing? Tell me the brand + the field, and I'll re-extract from the source FDD.
  • Adding a brand. Brand not in the database? Send the franchisor name + a state portal link if you have one, and I'll add it to the queue.
  • Press / interview requests. Happy to talk franchise data, FDD methodology, or industry trends. Include the publication and angle.
  • Partnership inquiries. Affiliate networks, franchise consultants, SBA lenders — see the affiliate disclosure first.
  • General feedback. What's missing, what's confusing, what would make this site more useful to you.

What I can't help with

  • Direct franchise recommendations. I don't tell people which franchise to buy — the data is the data; the decision is yours and your attorney's.
  • Forecasting your specific outlet's earnings. Item 19 is the authoritative source for any forecast; I can't legally project your returns (FTC §436.5(s)).
  • Connecting you with a specific franchisor. Use the franchisor's official website for that.

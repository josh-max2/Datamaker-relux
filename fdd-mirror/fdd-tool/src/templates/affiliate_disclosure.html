Affiliate disclosure

Required by the FTC, written in plain English.

What this means

FranchiseDepth is funded primarily by affiliate commissions paid when our visitors take certain actions on partner websites. Two specific CTAs on brand pages are affiliate links:

  • "Talk to a franchise consultant" — routes to franchise broker partners (e.g., FlexOffers, IFPG-member brokers). We may receive a commission if the broker matches you with a franchise opportunity.
  • "Get pre-qualified for SBA financing" — routes to SBA-preferred lenders (e.g., ApplePie Capital, Boefly, FranFund via lender networks). We may receive a commission if you ultimately close a loan.

What this does not mean

  • The price you pay is not affected. Affiliate commissions come from the partner's marketing budget, not from a markup on you.
  • We do not accept payment to include or rank brands. Every brand on this site is here because it filed a publicly-available FDD that we extracted. There is no paid placement.
  • We do not endorse the franchises themselves. Every brand page is data summary plus context — buyers make their own decisions.
  • The data we publish (Item 5/6/7/19/20 figures) comes directly from state-filed FDDs and is not influenced by any commercial relationship.

How we keep these separate

Our extraction pipeline is open source (see github.com/josh-max2/Parser). It runs against public state portals (Wisconsin DFI, Minnesota CARDS, etc.) and processes whatever filings exist. There is no "preferred brand" code path. The data presented matches the source filings; any number that disagrees with the source PDF (linked on every brand page) should be considered a transcription error and reported.

Why we disclose this prominently

The FTC's Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255) require clear and conspicuous disclosure when a publisher receives material connection (including affiliate commissions) for promoting a product or service. We display this disclosure:

  • In the footer of every page (always visible)
  • As an italicized note above every affiliate CTA on brand pages (next to the recommendation, not buried)
  • On this dedicated disclosure page (linked from both the footer and inline disclosures)

Questions?

Reach out via the contact information on the About page. We'd rather over-communicate on this than fall short of FTC requirements.

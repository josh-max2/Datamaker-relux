Franchise buying guides

Plain-English guides to evaluating a franchise opportunity. Every recommendation here cross-references the actual data we've extracted from publicly filed FDDs.

Available guides

  • Understanding FDD Item 19

    What Item 19 actually tells you, what's optional, how to read the cohort labels, and how to use the numbers without getting cherry-picked by the franchisor's framing.

  • Understanding FDD Item 7 (total investment)

    The line items that surprise first-time buyers, what's not in the range, and how to use the High end honestly. Why the headline "$150K to open" usually isn't enough.

  • How to validate franchise earnings before signing

    The single highest-leverage research you can do — direct calls with current franchisees from the Item 20 list. Who to call, what to ask, what to trust. 10 questions every call should cover.

  • Understanding FDD Item 20 (outlet activity + closures)

    How to read closure rate vs transfers, what the state-by-state table reveals about brand stability, and why "outlets closed" is a catch-all that mixes very different events.

  • How long does it take to break even on a franchise?

    Operational breakeven vs investment breakeven vs wage-replacement breakeven — three different things that get called the same word. How to compute defensible numbers from Items 7 and 19, and the typical ranges we see by category.

  • SBA 7(a) franchise financing: how eligibility actually works now

    The SBA Franchise Directory was discontinued in 2023. Eligibility is now decided per-lender, per-deal. What the lender actually checks, how to find out if a brand passes, and how to stress-test the deal economics.

  • What's negotiable in a franchise agreement?

    The financial terms (initial fee, royalty structure, renewal, territory) are negotiable far more often than franchisors admit. The brand-protection clauses aren't. How to tell the difference and where leverage comes from.

  • How to evaluate a franchise discovery day

    Discovery day is when the franchisor evaluates you — and when you should be evaluating them. Specific questions to ask each leadership role, behaviors to observe, red flags that warrant walking out, and the 72-hour rule for the inevitable post-day euphoria.

More guides coming: how to negotiate franchise agreement terms, how to choose your protected territory, and how to evaluate franchisor support quality during discovery day.

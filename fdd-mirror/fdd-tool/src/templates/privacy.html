Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ generated_at }}.

FranchiseDepth (the "Site") is an independent project. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, why, and what choices you have. The short version: we collect as little as possible, we don't sell personal data, and you can opt out of optional tracking at any time via the "Cookie preferences" link in the footer.

What we collect

1. Cookies + local storage (functional, always on)

We store your cookie-consent choice in your browser's localStorage under the key fd_consent_v1. This is necessary so we don't re-prompt you on every visit. No personal data is stored — just a JSON record of which categories you accepted.

2. Anonymous analytics (only with your consent)

If you click Accept all or enable Analytics in the customize modal, we load two analytics services. Both are gated by the same consent toggle — accepting analytics enables both; rejecting disables both. Neither receives any data until you accept.

Google Analytics 4 (GA4) receives:

  • Page URL, referrer, language
  • Approximate location (country/region from IP, never precise)
  • Device type, browser, operating system
  • Anonymous interaction events: page views, scroll depth, outbound link clicks, lead-form submissions
  • A pseudonymous client identifier generated by GA4 (no name, no email)

GA4 data is retained by Google for 14 months (the shortest retention period available).

Microsoft Clarity receives anonymous heatmap + session-replay data:

  • Mouse movements, clicks, scroll behavior
  • Page URLs viewed in your session
  • Device type, browser, operating system
  • A pseudonymous session identifier (no name, no email)
  • Clarity automatically masks text input fields, so anything you type into a form (email, message, etc.) is not recorded in the replay — it appears as redacted blocks

Clarity data is retained for up to 13 months. If you reject analytics, Clarity is never loaded and no session data is captured.

3. Lead-capture form submissions (only if you submit)

If you submit the email-capture form on a brand page or the dedicated lead-magnet page, we collect:

  • Your email address (required, to send the requested resource)
  • Brand context (which page you submitted from)
  • Timestamp of submission

Form submissions are routed through Formspree, a privacy-respecting form-handler service. Formspree's privacy policy applies to the transmission. Once received, your email is added to our newsletter list (you can unsubscribe at any time from the footer of any email we send).

What we don't collect

  • Names, addresses, phone numbers, or any personally-identifying information beyond what you voluntarily submit
  • Payment information (we don't sell anything directly)
  • Precise geolocation
  • Browsing history outside this site
  • Health, biometric, or other sensitive personal data
  • Data from children under 13 (this site is intended for adult business buyers)

Your rights

Under GDPR (EU/EEA residents)

  • Access — request a copy of any personal data we hold (your email, if you submitted the form)
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten") — request deletion of your data
  • Restriction — limit how we process your data
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection — object to processing
  • Withdraw consent — at any time via the "Cookie preferences" link in the footer

Under CCPA + CPRA (California residents)

  • Right to know what personal information is collected, used, shared, or sold
  • Right to delete personal information held about you
  • Right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell personal data, but you have this right regardless)
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising these rights

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@franchisedepth.com. We aim to respond within 30 days.

How long we retain data

  • Cookie consent record: until you clear it via "Cookie preferences" or your browser's storage settings
  • GA4 analytics data: 14 months (Google's minimum retention)
  • Microsoft Clarity session data: up to 13 months
  • Lead-form submissions: until you unsubscribe or request deletion

Children

This site is intended for adults considering franchise investment. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, email hello@franchisedepth.com and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when we add new features, change third-party services, or in response to regulatory requirements. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent change. Material changes (new categories of data collected, new third parties) will be announced in the footer banner.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests: hello@franchisedepth.com.

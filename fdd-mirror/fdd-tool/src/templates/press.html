How to cite

If you're using our data in your own writing or research:

  • Brand-specific data: "According to {{ site_name }}'s analysis of [Brand]'s [YEAR] FDD ([State] filing)…" — link to the specific brand page.
  • Comparisons: "{{ site_name }} compared [Brand A] and [Brand B] head-to-head…" — link to the specific compare page.
  • Methodology: "{{ site_name }} extracts Item 7, Item 19, and Item 20 data from publicly filed FDDs, with source-document deep links on every number." — link to the methodology page.

We don't require permission for fair-use citation. We do appreciate a link.

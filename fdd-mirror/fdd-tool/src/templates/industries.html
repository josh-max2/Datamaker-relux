Why we organize this way

Franchise opportunities cluster by operational category. A cleaning franchise looks nothing like a remodeling franchise on the inputs side — labor model, ticket size, capital intensity, sales cycle — even when their headline Item 7 ranges overlap. Comparing within a category surfaces the trade-offs honestly; comparing across categories without that context tends to mislead.

We currently cover the home-services vertical in depth (cleaning, handyman, plumbing, restoration, remodeling, outdoor, roofing, HVAC, moving). Food service, fitness, retail, and B2B-services verticals are next — see the methodology page for the extraction process + refresh schedule.

How "recent" works on this site

Each FDD lists an "issuance date" — the day the franchisor finalized that year's disclosure document. The franchisor must use that filing as the basis for any franchise sale until they issue a new one. Newer filings reflect the franchisor's most current operating economics, fee structure, and outlet performance.

We re-scrape state portals on a rolling basis. When a franchisor files a new FDD, we replace our stored version and regenerate the brand page. The brand page byline shows when the source was last verified and when the page was last regenerated.

    Highest reported earnings — Food Quick Service franchises

    85 food quick service franchise brands that disclose Item 19 financial performance, ranked by their headline reported revenue from their most recent FDD. Cohort labels are shown so you know what's being compared. This is not a profitability ranking — most franchisors report revenue, not net profit, so this page reflects what they chose to disclose.

    Page updated 2026-05-20

    How to read this ranking honestly:

    • Brands are ranked by the most representative number they disclosed — preferring all-franchised cohorts over performance-anchored ones (Top 10% / Top 25%).
    • When a brand only discloses for a top-performer cohort, we say so in the Cohort column. Those numbers describe top performers, not typical buyers.
    • Revenue is not profit. A $1M revenue outlet with 4% margin nets $40K. The same revenue at 18% margin nets $180K. The franchisor decides whether to disclose the profit side.
    • Item 19 disclosure is voluntary. Brands not on this list either don't disclose Item 19 at all, or disclose only non-revenue metrics. Read the Item 19 guide for context.

    What this page does not tell you

    This page ranks brands by reported revenue from a single cohort. Reasonable next questions a buyer should ask before drawing conclusions:

    • What does net profit look like at that revenue? Apply industry-typical margins, or look for a brand that discloses net margin. See each brand's arithmetic breakeven scenario on its brand page.
    • What's the cost to reach that revenue? Compare against Item 7 total investment.
    • What's the distribution? Item 19 medians can mask wide variance. Quartile data (P25 / P75) tells you whether the median is realistic for new franchisees.
    • How many outlets are in the cohort? Small samples (under 10 outlets) have high noise.
    • What did current franchisees actually earn? Use the validation-call playbook to ask real franchisees in Item 20 directly.

    Methodology

    For each brand, we picked the most representative revenue figure following this preference order:

    1. Broad cohort first: all_franchised → all_company_owned → tenure-based (open_X_plus_mo) → top-performer (performance_anchored)
    2. Metric: gross_sales preferred over total_revenue (gross sales is more comparable across brands)
    3. Statistic: median preferred over average (less skewed by top performers)

    When only a top-performer cohort was disclosed, we used it but flagged it. See the methodology page for the full extraction pipeline and the Item 19 guide for cohort interpretation.
